## Template System Overview

### What Are Templates?

Templates are pre-configured professional lighting setups that can be applied to your scene in one click. Each template includes:

- **Multiple lights** positioned professionally
- **Optimized properties** (power, size, color)
- **Intelligent positioning** based on your subject
- **Industry-standard configurations** used by professionals

### Template Categories

LumiFlow includes **40+ templates** organized into 4 categories:

| Category | Templates | Best For |
|----------|-----------|----------|
| **Studio & Commercial** | 12 | Product photography, portraits, e-commerce |
| **Dramatic & Cinematic** | 10 | Film, storytelling, dramatic moods |
| **Environment & Realistic** | 12 | Outdoor scenes, natural lighting |
| **Utilities & Single Lights** | 6 | Building blocks, custom setups |

### Template Dialog Options

When applying any template, you'll see a dialog with options:

**Clear Existing Lights**
- ✅ Checked: Removes all existing lights before applying template
- ⬜ Unchecked: Keeps existing lights, adds new ones

**Preserve Existing Lights**
- ✅ Checked: Keeps existing lights unchanged
- ⬜ Unchecked: Allows template to modify existing lights (rare)

**Auto-Scale**
- ✅ Checked: Automatically adjusts light distance/size based on scene
- ⬜ Unchecked: Uses default template distances

**Recommendation:** For first-time use, check "Clear Existing Lights" and "Auto-Scale".

## Studio & Commercial Templates (12)

Professional lighting for product photography, portraits, and commercial work.

### 1. Three-Point Setup

**Description:** Classic 3-point lighting (Key, Fill, Rim)  
**Lights:** 3  
**Use Case:** Standard portrait, interview, talking head

**Light Configuration:**
- **Key Light:** 45° front-side, 300W, Area light (soft)
- **Fill Light:** Opposite side, 100W, very soft
- **Rim Light:** 135° back, 150W, highlights edges

**Best For:**
- Beginners learning lighting fundamentals
- Safe, professional look for any subject
- Interview and documentary setups

**Tips:**
- Adjust key-to-fill ratio for mood (3:1 is standard)
- Move rim light for different edge highlights
- Increase fill power for less drama

---

### 2. Product Shot Basic

**Description:** Simple 2-light product photography  
**Lights:** 2  
**Use Case:** E-commerce, simple products

**Light Configuration:**
- **Key Light:** 45° angle, 200W
- **Fill Light:** Lower power, opposite side

**Best For:**
- E-commerce product images
- Clean, evenly lit products
- Simple objects without complex details

**Tips:**
- Use with white background shader
- Position camera straight on
- Adjust key light with Highlight mode for glossy products

## How to Apply Templates

### Basic Application

1. **Select Subject**
   - Click on object(s) you want to light
   - Multiple objects can be selected

2. **Choose Template**
   - Click template button in panel
   - Or browse template categories

3. **Configure Dialog**
   - ✅ Clear Existing Lights (recommended first time)
   - ✅ Auto-Scale (recommended)
   - Click OK

4. **Lights Created**
   - Check Outliner for new lights
   - View in camera mode (`Numpad 0`)
   - Switch to Material Preview (`Z` key → Material Preview)

### Advanced Options

**Multiple Objects:**
- Select all objects before applying template
- Template calculates bounds of all objects
- Lights position based on group center

**Custom Scaling:**
- Uncheck Auto-Scale
- Lights use default template distances
- Manually adjust after application

**Preserve Mode:**
- Check "Preserve Existing Lights"
- Template adds to existing setup
- Good for layering multiple templates

## Template Customization

### After Applying a Template

Templates are just starting points - customize freely!

**1. Reposition Lights**
- Use positioning modes (Highlight, Normal, Target, etc.)
- Manually move lights with `G` key
- Use Orbit mode to rotate around subject

**2. Adjust Properties**
- Use Smart Controls (`MMB` drag)
- Modify power, distance, temperature
- Change light size and softness

**3. Add/Remove Lights**
- Add more lights with `Ctrl+Shift+A`
- Delete unwanted lights (`X` key)
- Duplicate lights (`Shift+D`)

**4. Color Grading**
- Use Temperature control for subtle color shifts
- Or manually set RGB color in light properties
- Mix warm and cool for visual interest

**5. Light Linking**
- Use `Ctrl+Shift+X` to link lights to specific objects
- Create groups for complex scenes
- Selective lighting control

### Saving Custom Templates

Unfortunately, saving custom templates as new presets is not yet available in v1.0.0.

**Workaround:**
- Save different .blend files as your template library
- Append lights from other .blend files (`File > Append`)
- Use Blender's Asset Browser (Blender 3.0+)

## Tips for Template Success

### Choose the Right Template
- Match template to your subject and mood
- Read descriptions carefully
- Preview similar work online

### Start Simple
- Begin with basic templates
- Master fundamentals before complex setups
- Gradually add complexity

### Understand the Lights
- Note which light does what
- Observe key, fill, rim relationships
- Learn why lights are positioned where they are

### Experiment Freely
- Templates are non-destructive
- Try different templates on same scene
- Mix and match lights from different templates

### Learn from Templates
- Study professional positioning
- Note power ratios between lights
- Understand color temperature choices
